# Privacy Policy - Editora PDFEdit

_Last updated: 2026_

Editora PDFEdit is developed by **Kanishka Meddegoda**
([LinkedIn](https://lk.linkedin.com/in/kanishka-in)).

**Summary: your files and data never leave your computer.**

- **What the app does with your files.** PDFs and images you choose are processed on your own
  device. They are held in memory only (uploads for up to one hour of inactivity, results for
  30 minutes) and are discarded automatically. The app writes a file to disk only when you
  download a result.
- **What the app collects.** Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no
  telemetry and no crash reporting. The app does not send any information to the developer or
  to any third party.
- **Network use.** The app runs a small web server that is reachable only from your own
  computer (127.0.0.1). It makes no internet connections itself. If you click a link such as
  the developer's LinkedIn page, it opens in your normal web browser and that website's own
  privacy policy applies.
- **OCR.** Text recognition runs offline using the Tesseract engine that is included in the
  app; the language data is stored inside the app.
- **Children.** The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.
- **Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the app update.

Contact: via the developer's LinkedIn profile above.
